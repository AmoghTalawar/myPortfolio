About Me

Hi, I'm Devanshu.

Product Designer from India, I design experiences that feel effortless, blending technology seamlessly into everyday life so it becomes not just functional, but truly delightful. My work is driven by an optimistic, exploratory lens, a reflection of my name, which means "Sun God."

My Roots & Foundation

Raised in the Himalayan city of Dehradun and trained at NID Ahmedabad. Growing up, my love for design, storytelling, and movies shaped how I think and create. My design education taught me to question conventions, embrace ambiguity, and design with strong intent.

What I Do

I've crafted engaging digital experiences for the gaming space at Games24x7 in Bangalore, while also freelancing with diverse brands like Amazon Prime, Impact stories, Teqtis, and NEFSP to sharpen my visual and strategic thinking.

The Future of Tech

Fascinated by intelligent systems, I've completed a Product Management certification in Generative and Agentic AI from BITSOM. I love bridging design with business strategy to build smarter, human centered products.

Off-Screen

I am a storyteller at heart. When I'm not designing, I’m traveling, exploring Indian mythology, sketching, or writing illustrated fiction.
